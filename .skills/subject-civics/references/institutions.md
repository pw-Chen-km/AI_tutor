# Institutions, Rights & Government Structure

## Output Shape
- `## Prompt`: 描述制度或權責情境。
- `## Required`: 要求辨識機關或程序。
- `## Answer`: 給出對應制度要素。
- `## Rationale`: 連結法理與職權邏輯。

## Example

```markdown
## Prompt
A city council passes a bylaw that conflicts with national law.

## Required
Identify which legal level prevails and why.

## Answer
National law prevails due to legal hierarchy.

## Rationale
Subnational rules cannot override higher-level statutory authority.
```
