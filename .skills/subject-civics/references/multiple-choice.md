# Civics Multiple Choice

## Output Shape
- `## Question`: 單一明確題幹，聚焦一個概念。
- `## Options`: 4 個選項（A-D），語法平行且長度接近。
- `## Answer`: 僅標示一個正確選項。
- `## Rationale`: 1-2 句指出判斷依據。

## Example

```markdown
## Question
Which factor most directly increases evaporation rate?

## Options
A. Lower temperature
B. Larger exposed surface area
C. Lower wind speed
D. Higher humidity

## Answer
B

## Rationale
A larger surface area allows more molecules to escape per unit time.
```
