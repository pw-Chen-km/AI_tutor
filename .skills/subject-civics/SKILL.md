## Workflow

1. Name jurisdiction or level of government when relevant.
2. Identify stakeholders and institutional rules from the source.
3. Separate descriptive (what is) from normative (what should be) prompts.

## Avoid

- Partisan framing not present in teaching materials
- Legal conclusions that require expertise beyond the source
