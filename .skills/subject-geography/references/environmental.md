# Environmental & Physical Geography

## Output Shape
- `## Context`: 提供自然或環境現象。
- `## Question`: 指定成因、影響或應對。
- `## Answer`: 給出核心判斷。
- `## Rationale`: 以地理機制支撐。

## Example

```markdown
## Context
A basin region shows severe winter smog despite stable emissions.

## Question
What atmospheric condition most likely explains pollutant buildup?

## Answer
Temperature inversion.

## Rationale
Inversion suppresses vertical mixing and traps pollutants near the surface.
```
