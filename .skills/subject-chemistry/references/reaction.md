# Reactions, Mechanisms, and Nomenclature

## Output Shape
- `## Prompt`: 描述反應物、條件或機構。
- `## Required`: 指定產物、機構或命名任務。
- `## Answer`: 提供正確結果。
- `## Rationale`: 說明反應邏輯。

## Example

```markdown
## Prompt
Predict the major product of electrophilic addition to propene with HBr.

## Required
Provide product name and regioselectivity basis.

## Answer
2-bromopropane is the major product.

## Rationale
Markovnikov addition places Br on the more substituted carbon.
```
