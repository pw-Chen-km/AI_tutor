# Conceptual Physics Questions

## Output Shape
- `## Question`: 針對物理概念提出判斷。
- `## Context`: 給定必要情境。
- `## Answer`: 明確結論。
- `## Rationale`: 說明概念依據。

## Example

```markdown
## Context
A puck moves on near-frictionless ice with constant velocity.

## Question
What is the net horizontal force on the puck?

## Answer
Approximately zero.

## Rationale
Constant velocity implies zero net force by Newton's first law.
```
