# Biological Processes & Pathways

## Output Shape
- `## Prompt`: 指定生物過程與條件。
- `## Required`: 要求機制或結果判讀。
- `## Answer`: 給出關鍵步驟。
- `## Rationale`: 對應生理或分子依據。

## Example

```markdown
## Prompt
Explain how stomata closure affects photosynthesis on a hot afternoon.

## Required
State one mechanism and one consequence.

## Answer
Stomata closure lowers CO2 intake, which can reduce carbon fixation rate.

## Rationale
Reduced internal CO2 limits Calvin cycle throughput.
```
