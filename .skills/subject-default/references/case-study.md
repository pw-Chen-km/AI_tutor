# Case Study (Generic)

## Output Shape
- `## Scenario`: 3-6 行情境資料。
- `## Question`: 1 個分析問題。
- `## Expected Points`: 3-5 個判斷重點。
- `## Answer`: 結論 + 依據。

## Example

```markdown
## Scenario
A coastal city records frequent flooding after intensive upstream construction and wet-season storms.

## Question
What is the most plausible driver, and what policy response is appropriate?

## Expected Points
- Surface runoff increase
- Drainage overload
- Mitigation policy

## Answer
Runoff intensified by land-use change is the likely driver; retention basins and zoning controls are suitable responses.
```
