# Historical Essays

## Output Shape
- `## Prompt`: 提出論述題目與立場任務。
- `## Structure`: 指定段落骨架。
- `## Answer`: 提供示範段落。
- `## Scoring`: 內容、論證、語言三向度。

## Example

```markdown
## Prompt
Argue whether public transport subsidies should increase in large cities.

## Structure
Claim -> two supporting reasons -> conclusion.

## Answer
Public transport subsidies should increase because they reduce congestion and improve access for low-income workers. While funding is finite, targeted subsidies yield broad social returns in mobility and emissions reduction.

## Scoring
- Clear claim: 1
- Evidence-based reasoning: 2
- Coherent structure: 1
```
