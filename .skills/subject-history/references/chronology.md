# Chronology & Causal Sequences

## Output Shape
- `## Prompt`: 提供歷史事件集合。
- `## Required`: 要求排序或因果鏈。
- `## Answer`: 給出正確時序。
- `## Rationale`: 指出關鍵轉折。

## Example

```markdown
## Prompt
Order the events: Industrialization, urban migration surge, modern labor law reforms.

## Required
Give the most plausible sequence.

## Answer
Industrialization -> urban migration surge -> labor law reforms

## Rationale
Economic restructuring precedes social pressure and then policy response.
```
